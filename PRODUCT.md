# Through Frost and Myth Atlas

TFAM Atlas is the companion world and reading experience for Leonardo Ravenna’s historical fantasy Through Frost and Myth. Readers explore authored factions, characters, military, places and Vestigium, then discover more through published chronicles. It is a story world, not a strategy-game simulation.

The owner’s direction is cinematic imperial fantasy: expansive subject-specific artwork, elegant typography, confident composition and dynamic, deliberately choreographed interaction. Exploration can be theatrical; prose stays stable. Existing artwork is valuable content, not a substitute for fabricated 3D models.

Global destinations are World, Vestigium and Chronicles. Faction destinations are Overview, Atlas, Characters, Military and Landmarks. Castilian is the current content focus; Moor is the second initially available faction. Preserve the existing organizational roster and actual fields/functions.
