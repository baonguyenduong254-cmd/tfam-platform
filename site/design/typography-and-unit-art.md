# TFAM typography and military folios

Approved direction: hand-painted 2.5D military scenes, ink engraving and matte colour washes on warm yellow/ivory parchment. Every faction has a distinct representative unit. Preserve the illustrated faces and silhouettes, period weapons and faction colours; avoid photographic skin, CGI and glossy rendering. Original generated PNGs are encoded as WebP at quality 88 for the site. Unit illustrations use `/units/parchment/` so the new artwork is distinct from the previous assets.

Vietnamese headings use Cormorant Garamond Semibold and reading text uses Alegreya. English headings use IM FELL English and reading text uses Elstob. The title logo remains a separate image. Font files are hosted locally, with their OFL licenses in `public/fonts/`. Vietnamese glyph coverage is preserved in normal and italic faces; Cormorant and Alegreya provide fallback coverage for Vietnamese proper names in English text.

Sources: Google Fonts repository for Cormorant Garamond, Alegreya and IM FELL English; the official googlefonts/elstob repository for Elstob. Generated illustration prompts are retained in `unit-parchment-prompts.json` for consistent future additions.
