# Detailed regional Atlas

The chart keeps the existing regional framing, year selection and fullscreen. Neighboring territories provide quiet geographic context. The selected realm supplies all city marks, the heraldic device and the physical geography overlay.

The [official Grand Cathay chart](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/1r2bPK7y/grand-cathay-is-mapped-for-the-first-time-in-warhammer-the-old-world/) informed the hierarchy of small engraved buildings, olive forest points and subdued rivers. [Jared Blando's commissioned Trench Crusade map](https://jaredblando.com/prints/p/trenchcrusademaprint) informed the separation of heraldry, labels and architecture. TFAM uses original symbol paths and its existing Castilian flag.

The city register uses the TFAM atlas catalogue and year-specific control. [Gibraltar](https://www.geonames.org/2411585/gibraltar.html) and [Ceuta](https://www.geonames.org/6362987/ceuta.html) use GeoNames city anchors for the authored fortress and naval works dossiers. Callouts can offset a symbol while the leader retains its source position. The fortified capital, town, fortress, port and landmark use the same shapes in the legend and on the map.

Natural Earth centerlines and elevations retain their sources and data selection notes in site/lib/atlas-geography.json. The forest marks represent sourced points in Valsaín and Muniellos. They do not represent forest extents or a historical vegetation survey. Geography is clipped to the selected year's territory, with no effect on the hit regions or page scroll.
